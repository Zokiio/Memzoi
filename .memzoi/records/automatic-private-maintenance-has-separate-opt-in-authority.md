# Automatic private maintenance has separate opt-in authority

Automatic private maintenance is a separate opt-in, versioned local maintenance policy over selected runtime records, regardless of origin integration. Event-local exact-duplicate no-op, bounded provenance attachment, and application of a direct typed owner correction belong to the triggering event. Lifecycle renewal, retention extension, supersession, consolidation, and contradiction resolution require an exact owner-authorized action; corpus-wide maintenance requires its own grant. Ambiguous truth selection never runs automatically, destructive redaction or deletion requires exact owner authorization, and repository materialization remains a separately authorized repository operation under #99 and #101. The initial writer surface is the explicit CLI materialization command.
