# Keep canonical records compact and proposal packets reviewable

Proposal packets are verbose Git-reviewable decision artifacts, canonical records are compact durable memory, and runtime indexes are generated projections. Curate one record per durable concept or reusable cluster rather than preserving every small observation or raw extraction as repository memory.
