# Unresolved high-confidence contradictions suppress automatic recall

Target contract: an accepted high-confidence unresolved contradiction over claim-bearing records with overlapping scope, effective time, and applicability makes every incompatible member ineligible for unqualified automatic recall. The records remain stored, active, inspectable, and auditable; a derived, rebuildable conflict overlay records member identities, detector/version, incompatibility confidence, reason codes, resolution state, and recall effect. Detection confidence is not truth confidence: Memzoi must not select a winner from recency, retrieval score, record confidence, or contradiction confidence alone, nor automatically merge, supersede, tombstone, redact, or delete members. A valid authoritative temporal update, conditionally distinct claims, and episodic reports have separate treatment. The invariant applies to every read surface; an authorized explicit inspection may expose the members and evidence, while an automatic result may emit only a bounded, authorization-controlled notice when relevant. A persistent conflict relation enables read-time suppression without maintenance-write authority; authority to establish or resolve the relation remains separate.
